How to play

The two pictures look almost identical, but the right one hides 7 differences. Click a difference in either picture and it is ringed in both. Find all 7 to finish the scene before the pace bar fills — beat the par time if you can. Scene 2 hides smaller, quieter changes than Scene 1, so the hunt gets harder.

Mouse: click a spot. Keyboard: Tab to a picture, move the dashed cursor with the arrow keys (Shift = bigger steps), press Enter to mark, H for a hint, R to restart. Hints are limited to 3 per scene, and wrong clicks count as mistakes.

Runs fully offline from these files. No accounts, no tracking, no network.
